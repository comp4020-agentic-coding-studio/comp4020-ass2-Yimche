# Window Managers as Worldview

Week 3. Where windows go, and who is allowed to move them.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## A layout policy is a constitution

It looks like plumbing. It decides who arranges the screen, you or the machine.

---

## Two theories of work

- **tiling** holds that space is scarce and allocated by rule, keyboard over mouse
- **floating** holds that a desktop is a desk, that overlap is natural
- one trusts the machine to arrange, the other trusts you

---

## What the choice costs

Gaps, borders, and workspaces are not decoration; they are the layout policy made
visible. Picking a manager is picking how much you value control over
convenience, ritual over improvisation.

---

## The hardest decision to reverse

The palette can change in an afternoon. The manager you learn your hands into is
the choice you will still be defending in week 12.

---

# Pick a theory, not a theme

The layout says more about you than the colours will.
