Week 5. You already know more than you use.

## A scheme is not a system

A scheme picks nice colours. A system survives contact with a new application.

---

## The constraints you cannot negotiate

- contrast decides whether your theme is still readable by lunchtime
- a palette needs restraint more than range
- accessibility is a design discipline, not an afterthought

---

## The convergence

Almost every setup you admire is, underneath, Gruvbox or Catppuccin or a cousin.
This is worth taking seriously rather than mocking.

## Why they keep winning

They solved the real problem, legibility across dozens of unrelated programs,
better than the scheme you would invent in a weekend. The question is what it
means that taste, given freedom, keeps arriving at the same six schemes.
