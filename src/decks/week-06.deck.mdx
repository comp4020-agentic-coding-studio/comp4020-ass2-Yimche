# Typography and the Terminal

Week 6. The letters you stare at all day.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Most typography, least thought

The terminal is where type matters most and gets considered least.

---

## What monospace decides

- fixed metrics constrain the whole layout
- hinting and rendering make one font crisp here and muddy there
- patch fonts smuggle icons into the character grid, so a battery is also text

---

## The ligature argument

Programming ligatures fuse characters into one glyph, turning "not equal" into a
single mark. People find them elegant, or find them a lie about what the code
actually says.

---

## Legibility or vibes

A font can be chosen measured, boring, and correct, or for the feeling it gives
the screenshot. The best setups know which they are optimising and do not pretend
the two are the same.
