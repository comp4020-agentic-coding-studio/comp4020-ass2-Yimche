# Minimalism versus Maximalism

Week 9. Two congregations that do not worship together.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## One question, two answers

What is a desktop for when it is not being used?

---

## The two positions

- **minimalism**: the desktop should recede; what you remove, you have mastered
- **maximalism**: the screen is a canvas; density is generosity
- empty space as discipline, against a refusal to apologise for wanting more

---

## The honest and dishonest versions

- minimalism as clarity, against minimalism as fear of commitment
- maximalism as abundance, against maximalism as noise standing in for a decision
- every setup is one of these, done well or done as avoidance

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## This is philosophy, not preference

You will end the course in one church or the other.
