# The Stack Beneath the Skin

Week 2. What a desktop is made of, before you change any of it.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## "The desktop" is a committee

A display server, a compositor, a window manager, a bar, a launcher, a terminal,
and the apps on top. Each is a separate program that agreed to cooperate.

---

## Who does what

- **display server** hands out the screen and the input
- **compositor** paints windows, and owns your shadows and gaps
- **window manager** decides where windows go
- **bar and launcher** are just more clients, drawn on top

---

## X11 and Wayland

Two contracts, not two teams.

- X11 is old, permissive, endlessly scriptable, which is why the tooling assumes it
- Wayland is newer, stricter, and folds the compositor and display server together
- Neither is a moral choice; the rest of your setup lives inside the one you sign

---

## Why the screenshot lies

A photograph cannot tell you which stack produced it. The same look is reachable
from either contract, by different means. Know which program you are talking to
before a tutorial tells you to "just add this line".
