# The Status Bar

Week 7. The most honest object on the desktop.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## It promises information

How much of it does anyone actually read?

---

## The modules everyone adds

- the CPU temperature, updating every second
- the memory graph, the current track
- the weather in a city you are already standing in

Each was added because it could be. Each is read approximately never.

## The real design problem

- what earns a permanent place in your field of view
- what belongs in a menu you open on demand
- what is there only because the empty space felt wrong

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Restraint is the hard part

A dashboard for a life that does not generate that much data is easy. The
disciplined bar shows only what its owner will look at.

---

## Before next week

Build a bar, then remove every module you did not glance at for a whole day. Keep
a note of what you removed and how it felt.
