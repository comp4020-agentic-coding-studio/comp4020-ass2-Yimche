# The Productivity Paradox

Week 11. The lecture the course has been apologising for in advance.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Sold as productivity

A faster workflow, fewer keystrokes, a machine tuned to your hand. Does any of it
survive the arithmetic?

---

## Do the sums

The keybinding that saves two seconds a day, set up over an afternoon, pays for
itself in roughly eight months. Assuming you never touch it again. Which you
will, on the following Tuesday.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Procrastination that feels like work

The customised desktop is the most elaborate method ever devised for not doing
any work, and its genius is that it feels exactly like working.

---

## The point is not guilt

It is to say clearly what the hours bought: the ones that will pay themselves
back, the ones that bought only pleasure, and the ones that were procrastination
in a terminal.

---

## Before next week

Reconstruct, as best you can, how many hours this semester's setup has cost you.
Bring the number, however alarming, to the lab.
