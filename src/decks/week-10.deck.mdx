# The Dream of the Perfect Setup

Week 10. A new machine becomes the old one in a single command.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## The dream every ricer nurses

Capture the setup so perfectly that configuration is over forever.

---

## The reproducibility spectrum

- a folder you copy and pray over
- a bootstrap script that installs and links
- a fully declarative system that treats your desktop as a build artefact

---

## The tools that chase it

- dotfile managers and bare git repositories, the pragmatic middle
- symlink farms
- Nix and home-manager, the whole environment declared once and rebuilt anywhere

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Every step charges for its guarantee

Nix asks you to learn a language and a worldview to escape the chore of setup.
The horizon of "done" keeps receding.

---

## Before next week

Make your setup bootstrap on a fresh machine, real or virtual. Time how long it
takes and write down everything you had to fix by hand.
