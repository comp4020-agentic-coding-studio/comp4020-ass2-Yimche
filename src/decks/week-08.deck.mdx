# The Screenshot Economy

Week 8. The turn the course has been building toward.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## The screenshot is the artefact

The setup is merely the means. Accept that, and a lot of irrational behaviour
snaps into focus.

---

## Composition implies an audience

- the neofetch output posed just so
- the command chosen for how it looks, not what it does
- the wallpaper peeking through gaps sized for the camera

---

## The designed object

- framing, and what a good rice leaves out
- the staged terminal, doing something it never does
- the audience, who is usually not the person who took it

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## The Defence begins here

If the screenshot is the artefact, every choice visible in it is a claim you can
be asked to justify. Starting this week, you will be.

---

## Before next week

Photograph your setup as if for r/unixporn, then again as it actually looks on a
Tuesday. Bring both to the lab and be ready to account for the difference.
