The lab where a colour scheme has to become a colour system.

## Before the lab

Bring the palette you applied to your terminal and one other program, and the
place they disagreed about what "background" means.

## In the lab

We measure contrast rather than argue about it, then extend one palette across a
third and fourth program to find where a scheme breaks and a system holds. If you
chose Gruvbox or Catppuccin, defend it. If you rolled your own, defend that
harder.

## Afterwards

You leave with a palette that survives contact with new applications, and a
contrast figure you can stand behind.
