A rice is a solitary thing to build and a social thing to defend. This component
marks the second half: the semester of labs and crits that turns a private
configuration into something you can stand behind in front of other people.

## The brief

> Turn up, show your work, and take part in the room.

The labs are where the rice is made legible to someone other than you. Bringing
work in progress, reading other people's desktops back to them, and answering the
questions a screenshot cannot field are all skills the final gallery assumes you
already have. This mark is for practising them every week rather than saving them
for the end.

## What you submit

Nothing is uploaded for this component. It is assessed on presence and
contribution across the twelve labs, and it culminates in the [week twelve
gallery](../../sessions/12-gallery/), where the term's participation is read as a
whole. Consistent, honest engagement is what earns the marks; a single polished
appearance does not.
