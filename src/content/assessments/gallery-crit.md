The course is a studio, and it runs on the room. This component marks what
you contribute to it, week by week, and how you hold up at the end.

## The brief

> Show up to the crits with real work and real feedback, and defend your finished
> rice to the room at the final gallery.

Most of this mark is earned quietly, across the term, in the weekly lab. It
rewards bringing work before you have fallen in love with it, and giving feedback
that is specific enough to be useful and honest enough to be worth hearing. A crit
where everyone nods is a crit that helped no one.

## What you submit

There is no artefact to upload. The weekly portion is assessed on your presence
and contribution in the lab. The final portion is your presentation at the
week 12 gallery: setup up, screenshot shown, and you fielding the questions a
photograph cannot answer on its own.
