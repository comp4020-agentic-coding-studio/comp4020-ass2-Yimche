The central work of the course. Over the semester you build one desktop and make
it yours, and at the end you hand over the whole thing.

## The brief

> Build a complete, original, reproducible Linux desktop that is unmistakably
> yours, and stand behind every part of it.

A strong rice is not the busiest or the emptiest one in the room. It is the one
whose choices hang together: a window manager, palette, typeface, and bar that
argue for the same idea of what a desktop is for. Originality here does not mean
inventing everything from scratch, which nobody does. It means that the sum is
yours even where the parts were forked, and that a marker can tell the difference
between the decisions you made and the ones you inherited.

## What you submit

A running setup, presented at the gallery in week 12, and the repository that
regenerates it. The repository is marked separately as the Dotfiles Repository,
and the screenshot and its written defence as the Defence; here it is the desktop
itself, as a whole, that is judged.
