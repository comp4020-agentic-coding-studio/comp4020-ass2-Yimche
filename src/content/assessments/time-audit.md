The course's closing argument, written by you about yourself. Ricing is sold as
productivity; this essay tests the claim against the arithmetic.

## The brief

> Account honestly for the hours this setup cost you, and say plainly what they
> bought.

The customised desktop is the most elaborate method ever devised for not doing
any work, and its trick is that it feels exactly like working. This essay asks you
to see through that in your own case. Estimate the hours, sort them into the ones
that will pay themselves back, the ones that bought only the pleasure of the
doing, and the ones that were procrastination in a terminal, and then say what you
actually think about the total.

## What you submit

A short reflective essay. It is not marked on reaching a particular verdict, and
"it was worth every hour" is as acceptable a conclusion as "I would take most of
them back", provided the accounting behind it is honest and the number is one you
can defend.
