If the screenshot is the artefact, then every choice in it is a claim you can be
asked to justify. The Defence is where you make good on that in writing.

## The brief

> Submit the screenshot your rice exists for, and defend every deliberate choice
> it shows.

This is the component that grew out of a thin idea into a real one. A screenshot
by itself proves nothing; anyone can photograph a forked theme. The defence is the
proof that the choices were choices. Take the composed frame apart element by
element and say, for each, why it is that way: why this manager, this palette,
this typeface, this bar, this wallpaper, this empty space. Where a choice was
inherited or accidental, say so. A defence that claims intent it did not have is
worse than one that admits a decision was luck.

## What you submit

One composed screenshot and a written defence keyed to it, element by element.
Marks go chiefly to the quality of the reasoning, then to how completely the
frame is accounted for, and a real share to plain honesty about what you meant
and what you merely inherited.
