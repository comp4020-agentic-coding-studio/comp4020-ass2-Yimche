The screenshot is how ricing travels, and composing one is a craft in its own
right. This component marks that craft, and then asks you to tell the truth about
it.

## The brief

> Compose one screenshot worth posting, and one that admits what the desktop is
> like to live in.

The staged shot is the poster: the wallpaper, the open windows, the running
widgets, and the framing all arranged to argue for the desktop at its best. The
honest shot is its counterweight, the same machine mid-task with the clutter left
in. The pair is the point. A rice that only ever photographs well is hiding
something, and the gap between the two images is often where the interesting
decisions live.

## What you submit

Two images of the same desktop: a composed hero shot at full native resolution,
and an unstaged shot taken on an ordinary working day. Name what changed between
them, so the marker can read the staging as a set of choices rather than a trick.

The criteria below are weighted. Composition carries the most, because the poster
is the artefact the rest of the world sees; the honest pair is marked next,
because a screenshot you cannot stand behind is worth less than one you can.
