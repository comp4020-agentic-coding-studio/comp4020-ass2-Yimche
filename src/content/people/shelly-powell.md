Shelly runs the weekly lab. They maintain a dotfiles repository that other
students have cloned without asking, which they take as the highest form of
citation. Their interest is reproducibility: whether the thing you show the room
on Monday can be rebuilt from scratch on Friday.

Bring them work in progress rather than finished work. Feedback is more useful
before you have fallen in love with a decision than after.
