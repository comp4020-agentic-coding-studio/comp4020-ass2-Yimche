Urek tutors the weekly lab. Their subject is failure: the config that worked on
one machine and nowhere else, the theme that looked right until the sun moved,
the keybinding that fought the muscle memory it was meant to serve.

Bring them what broke. A rice that has never fallen over has not yet been asked
to do anything.
