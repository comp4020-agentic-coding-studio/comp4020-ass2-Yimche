Alec convenes the course and sets its weekly programme. They work on the
history and theory of personal computing environments, which is a respectable
way of saying they have thought too long about status bars. Their own setup has
been declared finished on at least four occasions.

Come to them with questions about the shape of the course, extensions, and any
choice you are trying to justify and cannot. They would rather you defend a
strange decision well than make a safe one you cannot explain.
