Marisol convenes the course and sets its weekly programme. She works on the
history and theory of personal computing environments, which is a respectable
way of saying she has thought too long about status bars. Her own setup has been
declared finished on at least four occasions.

Come to her with questions about the shape of the course, extensions, and any
choice you are trying to justify and cannot. She would rather you defend a
strange decision well than make a safe one you cannot explain.
